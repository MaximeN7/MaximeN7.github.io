Internships

Internship and temporary contract at Numix

During my second year in La Prépa des INP I realised a 6 weeks long internship in software development in the Numix company that led to a temporary contract of 5 weeks to bring my projects to completion.
During those 3 months, I worked on an Augmented Reality project where I developed the security system for the devices used in the experience.

Internship at IRIT

During my first year in ENSEEIHT I realised a 6 weeks long internship in software development and conception in the IRIT Laboratory.
During this time, I worked alongside another classmate on a Proof of Concept for an online platform aimed at transforming school exercises into a more engaging, game-like experience.

Professional Projects

Mission with N7Consulting and ALTEN

I am currently working on a few months mission with N7Consulting, the Junior Enterprise of ENSEEIHT, in collaboration with the company ALTEN.
My role involves, alongside with another developer and a pixel-artist, developing 2 small games for a mobile application specifically designed for children who have suffered from severe burns.
This project combines enhancement of technical skills in game development with a meaningful social impact, contributing to the well-being of young patients through gaming.
